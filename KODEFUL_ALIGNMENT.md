# Kodeful Specification Alignment

Review date: October 10, 2026
Baseline: IRIS-MVP-Developer-Packet-v1.1.zip, sent to Kodeful on August 20, 2026 (confirmed by the IRIS Developer Packet email). The accompanying IRIS Developer Briefing for Kodeful supports interpretation, not replacement of formal requirements.

## Authority And Scope

The packet's authority map assigns product doctrine to Canon, scope to PRD, behavior to FRD, entity meaning to CDM, evidence semantics to Validation Model, and visual identity to official assets/tokens. Several packet documents are explicitly draft/pending owner approval; this review does not turn them into an executed agreement or claim full production acceptance.

This remains the requested senior-living sales demo, not a reproduction of all 32 Cedar Valley screens or the complete MVP. Harborview is an intentional sector adaptation. No Cedar Valley facts, prototype source scripts, customer records, or production IRIS code were imported. Only the official dark-text logo asset is reused.

## Gaps Found In The Live Version

- Readiness numbers 67/72/64/59 imply unsupported precision, despite simulation labels.
- Four selectable findings conflict with the displayed seven open findings.
- Invented teal-gradient wordmark and heavy shadows differ from the official IRIS identity.
- Supporting evidence is plain text rather than inspectable records with provenance.
- Finding-to-action is explanatory text, without proposed/assigned/completed/validation distinctions in behavior.
- No plan lifecycle/version/review separation or clarification workflow.
- Activity percentages are presented under validation without distinct capability progression and evidence sufficiency.

## Deliberately Not Implemented

Production authentication, tenant/role enforcement, durable persistence, immutable audit, secure uploads/retrieval, live analysis, onboarding, full risk register, full training/exercise/incident authoring, notifications, billing, exports, and production infrastructure/security assurance remain outside this sales-demo scope. Session history is demonstrative, not a security audit trail. This public demo is not Kodeful's isolated security-testing sandbox and makes no SOC 2 claim.

## Release Verification
